Business Rules Mapping:
- R1: IST (UTC+05:30) date & overnight shift (shift_end <= shift_start). Whole-second truncation.
- R2: Late grace period 10:00 strict (>10 min late from shift_start, floored).
- R3: Overtime floored minutes >= 30; otherwise 0.
- R4: Work hours (punch_out - punch_in) in sec / 3600, 2 decimals ROUND_HALF_UP.
- R5: Half day = true when rounded work_hours < 4.50 (0.5 present day).
- R6: Presence statuses: PRESENT, WFH, ON_DUTY. Non-presence: ABSENT, LEAVE.
- R7: Working days Mon-Fri, joined_on for mid-month joiners.
- R8: Number rounding 2 decimals half-up, rates 4 decimals half-up.
- R9: Headcount joined_on <= period end (including zero logs).
- R10: Pagination: page >= 1, page_size default 20 max 100, total reflects filters.
