1. PHASE 5.1 — EMPLOYEE MONTHLY SUMMARY AGGREGATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Implemented `GET /analytics/employees/{emp_code}/monthly` backed by MongoDB aggregation.
- Matches employee by `emp_code`, returning 404 for unknown employees.
- Validates `month` parameter as `YYYY-MM` and derives exact IST calendar boundaries.
- Computed all metrics in MongoDB aggregation pipeline:
  * `working_days`: Computed via MongoDB date math (`$documents`, `$dateDiff`, `$dateAdd`, `$dayOfWeek`) for Monday-Friday calendar days from `max(month_start, joined_on)` through `month_end`.
  * `present_days`: Counts weekday records with `PRESENT`, `WFH`, or `ON_DUTY` status (`half_day: True` contributes 0.5; non-weekday logs excluded).
  * `leave_days`: Count of `LEAVE` status records.
  * `late_count`: Count of records with stored `late_minutes > 0`.
  * `total_late_minutes` & `total_overtime_minutes`: Grouped sums of stored derived fields (including weekend records).
  * `attendance_pct`: `(present_days / working_days) * 100` rounded to 2 decimal places (`ROUND_HALF_UP`), returning `null` when `working_days == 0`.
- Ensured zero Python loops for metrics or date iterations.
