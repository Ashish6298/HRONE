1. PHASE 6.1 — DEPARTMENT SUMMARY AGGREGATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Implemented `GET /analytics/departments/summary` using MongoDB aggregation across collections.
- Starts from `employees` with filter `joined_on <= month_end` (R9) to ensure employees with zero logs are counted in headcount and late joiners are excluded.
- Uses `$lookup` to attach matching monthly `attendance_logs` for each employee.
- Computes aggregated metrics grouped by department:
  * `headcount`: Total eligible employees in department.
  * `present_days`: Sum of weekday presence logs (`half_day` = 0.5).
  * `avg_work_hours`: Average `work_hours` across qualifying attendance records with non-null work_hours (average over records, not average of employee averages); `null` if none exist.
  * `late_count`: Count of records with `late_minutes > 0`.
  * `total_late_minutes`: Sum of `late_minutes`.
  * `leave_count`: Count of `LEAVE` records.
  * `on_duty_count`: Count of `ON_DUTY` records.
- Sorted by `department ASC`.

2. PHASE 6.2 — LATE LEADERBOARD WITH COMPETITION RANKING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Implemented `GET /analytics/leaderboard/late` using MongoDB aggregation.
- Aggregates monthly `attendance_logs` matching `late_minutes > 0` and groups by `emp_code` to calculate `total_late_minutes` and `late_count`.
- Joins with `employees` to retrieve employee `name` and `department`.
- Filters by `department` before ranking when specified.
- Implements standard competition ranking (1, 2, 2, 4) using MongoDB `$setWindowFields` and `$rank` ordered by `total_late_minutes DESC`.
- Applies limit after ranking (`rank <= limit`) ensuring all employees tied at the cutoff rank are included.
- Deterministic sort order: `total_late_minutes DESC`, then `emp_code ASC`.
