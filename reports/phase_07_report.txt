1. PHASE 7.1 — DEPARTMENT DAILY TREND AGGREGATION & MOVING AVERAGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Implemented `GET /analytics/departments/{department}/trend` backed by a MongoDB aggregation pipeline.
- Enforced validation rules:
  * 404 for unknown department.
  * 422 for `from > to` or date range exceeding 92 calendar days.
- **Aggregation Pipeline Features (Zero Python Loops)**:
  * **Gap-Filling & Date Generation**: Uses MongoDB `$documents`, `$dateDiff`, `$range`, and `$dateAdd` to generate exactly one document per calendar day in `[from, to]`, including weekends and zero-log days.
  * **Day-Specific Headcount**: Dynamically looks up department employees with `joined_on <= date` for each day (R9).
  * **Daily Presence & Late Counts**: Looks up department attendance records for the date to compute `present_count` (`half_day` = 0.5) and `late_count`.
  * **Attendance Rate**: `present_count / headcount` on working days with `headcount > 0` (0.0 on zero-log working days with headcount > 0; `null` on weekends or when `headcount == 0`).
  * **7-Day Moving Average**: Computed directly in MongoDB using `$setWindowFields` over `window: {documents: [-6, "current"]}` sorted by `date ASC`. Excludes `null` attendance rates from moving average calculation, naturally handling the shorter window for initial days inside the requested date range.
  * Applied R8 4-decimal half-up rounding for rates and 2-decimal rounding for counts.
